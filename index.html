02 / The studio

A slower kind of studio

We kept the flower shop’s tiled floor and big windows and built four chairs around them. Nobody is rushed and nothing is reused.

  • 01

    Hospital-grade hygiene

Good to know

How long does gel last?

Two to three weeks for most people. We remove it gently, without drilling the natural nail.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Send a picture with your booking and we'll plan the time it needs.

What if I'm running late?

Message us. Up to fifteen minutes we can usually keep the full service.

Is the aurora finish safe?

It's a cosmetic top coat with a fine shimmer pigment, cured like any gel.
